What the synthetic dataset shows

  • 4,200 fictional orders and 9,319 line items.
  • Highest-revenue month: December 2025, with PKR 7.32M net revenue.
  • Highest-revenue category: Electronics, with PKR 23.66M net revenue.
  • Highest modeled-profit category: Electronics, with PKR 6.39M.
  • Modeled channel margin ranges from 30.8% for Marketplace to 36.1% for Retail Store, a 5.3 percentage-point spread.

Questions to test with real data

  1. Does higher discounting increase orders enough to offset reduced margin?
  2. Do channel margin differences reflect discounting, product mix, or unmodeled fulfillment costs?
  3. Which categories contribute profit, not only revenue?
  4. Would customer retention and repeat-purchase analysis change the recommendations?

Limitations

Product costs are simplified. The analysis excludes shipping, inventory, taxes, returns, overhead, marketing spend, payment fees, and verified customer behavior. No causal claims are supported. Any business recommendation would need validation with real data.
