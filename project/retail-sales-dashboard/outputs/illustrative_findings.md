# Illustrative findings (synthetic data only)

> These are calculations from a fictional, generated dataset. They are practice findings, not evidence about a real retailer or client. Amounts are in Pakistani rupees (PKR).

- **Net revenue:** PKR 70,091,003 across 4,200 distinct orders.
- **Profit and margin:** PKR 23,913,053 profit at a 34.1% modeled margin.
- **Average order value:** PKR 16,688.
- **Highest-revenue month:** 2025-12 with PKR 7,316,264 net revenue.
- **Highest-revenue category:** Electronics with PKR 23,657,345 net revenue.
- **Highest-profit category:** Electronics with PKR 6,386,555 profit.
- **Channel margin spread:** Retail Store has the highest modeled channel margin (36.1%); Marketplace has the lowest (30.8%), a difference of 5.3 percentage points.

## Questions a real business should investigate

1. Does higher discounting increase order volume enough to offset the reduction in unit margin?
2. Are the differences in channel profitability driven by discounting, product mix, or fulfillment costs? (Fulfillment costs are not included in this exercise.)
3. Which categories produce both revenue and profit, rather than revenue alone?
4. Would repeat-customer and cohort analysis change the interpretation?

## Limitations

The dataset is synthetic and contains intentionally simplified costs. It omits taxes, shipping, returns, inventory, marketing spend, payment fees, and actual customer behaviour. No causal or client-impact claims should be made from these results.
