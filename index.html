Retail Sales Performance & Profitability

A fictional multi-channel retailer needs to understand where revenue comes from and whether that revenue is profitable. The workflow cleans transaction-line data, calculates KPIs, compares categories and channels, and provides a Power BI implementation guide with DAX measures.
